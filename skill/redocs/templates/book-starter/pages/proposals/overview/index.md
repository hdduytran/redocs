# Proposals

> TODO(owner): Each planned change gets its own page here (`proposals/<slug>`, kind `proposal`).

A proposal page states:

1. **Context** — what is wrong or missing today (link the current pages).
2. **Decision** — what changes, at which milestone.
3. **Impact** — entities added (`since`), removed (`until`) or replaced (`replaced_by`), and the
   pages that get a new variant for that milestone.
4. **Alternatives considered** and why they were rejected.
5. **Rollout** — the steps a developer team would take.

The generated **Changes** pages summarise entity differences between milestones automatically.
